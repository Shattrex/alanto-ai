Notes

Executive summary

Partnership Collaboration Proposal

Action Pro × Leafy Brew Coffee & Cafe

Action Pro proposes a same-day referral partnership with Leafy Brew Coffee & Cafe. A customer who purchases from either business may present that day’s invoice at the other and receive a discount percentage agreed by both partners. The offer runs in both directions.

It is explained on table stands, counter signs, and shared social posts. When Leafy Brew hosts or facilitates events, attendees can be included, with Action Pro extending the benefit across clothing, makeup, tumblers, crockery, slippers, hair accessories, wallets, and purses.

The offer

Same-day invoice, both ways, at a rate both businesses set before launch.

The promotion

Standees, counter signs, a shared “We Collaborate” mark, stories, and a launch post.

The agreement

A short MOU covering rate, duration, duties, events, and a periodic review.

The partnership is designed to increase footfall, strengthen mall visibility, and give customers a practical reason to visit both businesses.
